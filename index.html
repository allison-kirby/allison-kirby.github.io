About Me

Hi, I’m Allison! I’m a senior at the University of San Diego, majoring in Marketing with a minor in Entrepreneurship. I’m passionate about the intersection of creativity and strategy, especially when it comes to building authentic connections between brands and people. Through my internships and coursework, I’ve developed a strong foundation in digital marketing, social media strategy, and brand storytelling.

Beyond the classroom, I’m a Division I student-athlete on USD’s cross-country and track teams and an active member of the American Marketing Association and the Student-Athlete Advocacy Committee. Balancing academics, athletics, and creative work has taught me the value of discipline, time management, and finding joy in the process.

I’m always excited to connect with industry professionals and explore new opportunities to learn and contribute within the marketing space.
